ArmDojo 5-axis robot arm - printable parts (STL + STEP)
=======================================================

SO-101-derived desktop arm: 5 positioning axes + 1 gripper, six STS3215-family
servos (ST-3215-C018, 12 V, 1:345). Current build: R5B (reinforced wrist and
gripper). AD28 camera platform is optional.

STL/   print-oriented meshes (lowest Z = 0), one file per part.
STEP/  *_print.step solids in the same print orientation.
No servo CAD and no assembly files are included.

Servo IDs: ID1 base yaw, ID2 shoulder lift, ID3 elbow flex, ID4 wrist pitch,
ID5 wrist roll, ID6 gripper. All ID1-ID6 are 12 V C018 1:345; do not mix 7.4 V
STS3215 variants on the same 12 V bus.

PRINT NOTES
Common PLA settings (fit-verified on our prints): 0.20 mm layers, classic
walls, XY hole/contour compensation 0, elephant-foot 0.15 mm, outer wall
60 mm/s at 2000 mm/s2, 220 C nozzle, textured PEI 55 C, no brim.
TPU 95A liners: 230 C / 45 C, max 3.6 mm3/s, 3 walls, 7 bottom / 4 top,
no brim, print by object; on an A1 feed TPU from the external spool holder.

LIMITS
No payload rating. The arm is long for its servos: at full horizontal reach
the shoulder carries a large static load. Support the arm when testing, avoid
holding it fully stretched out for long periods, and keep early moves slow
with a low torque limit. Wrist roll is designed for -90..+90 deg (not
continuous). Gripper pads nearly touch at zero (~0.4 mm): calibrate the
gripper at low current and stop when the pads meet.

A matching Bambu Studio project for the Bambu Lab A1 0.4 mm nozzle is
provided in print-profile/ (5 plates, about 14 h 57 min and 303 g in total).
